PickSetGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: [set actual publication date when authorised]

1. About PickSetGo

PickSetGo is an independently developed fitness app that helps you generate workouts, follow training programmes and track your progress.

PickSetGo is designed to work without requiring an account. Your workout information is stored locally on your device rather than automatically synchronised with an online account.

This policy explains how your information is handled when you use PickSetGo or contact us.

2. Information stored on your device

PickSetGo stores information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Your equipment selections and workout preferences.
  • Generated and completed workouts.
  • Training history and progress.
  • Coach programmes and associated progress.
  • App settings and preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device.

PickSetGo uses it to generate workouts, personalise training programmes, track progress and remember your preferences.

Your locally stored training information is not automatically sent to the developer.

3. Backups and deleting information

You can create a backup of your PickSetGo information using the Backup & Restore feature.

You choose where to save your backup files and whether to share them. If you save a backup using a third-party cloud storage service, that service's privacy practices will apply.

PickSetGo does not provide automatic cloud backup or synchronisation.

Your saved training information remains in the app's local storage until it is deleted or that storage is cleared.

Clearing app or browser data, or uninstalling the app, may remove saved information. We recommend creating a backup first.

Backups you have saved separately will need to be managed or deleted by you.

4. Contacting support and providing feedback

If you contact PickSetGo by email, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your email address, message, screenshots and diagnostic information.

We use this information to:

  • Respond to support enquiries.
  • Investigate and resolve problems.
  • Consider feature suggestions and general feedback.
  • Improve the quality and reliability of PickSetGo.

Diagnostic information is not automatically sent to us. You choose whether to include it in a message.

We normally retain support and feedback correspondence, including attachments, for 12 months after an enquiry or feedback has been resolved or processed.

We review retained correspondence monthly to identify information due for deletion. Deletion is carried out following review and approval, so information may be held for a short additional period while that process is completed.

We may retain information for longer where reasonably necessary for an ongoing matter or to comply with a legal obligation.

We process support and feedback information on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding to users, investigating problems and improving PickSetGo, subject to applicable data protection law.

5. Hosting and third-party services

PickSetGo's web application is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

When you access PickSetGo through the website, GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the service.

Support and feedback emails are handled using Google's Gmail service. Google processes the information contained in emails and attachments in accordance with its applicable service arrangements.

GitHub and Google may process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. They publish information about their international data transfer arrangements and applicable safeguards in their privacy documentation.

You can find more information in:

  • GitHub's Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

PickSetGo does not sell your personal information or use advertising or analytics services in the current web application.

Spoken cues

PickSetGo uses your browser or device's speech service to provide spoken workout instructions and cues.

Depending on the voice and platform, spoken text may be processed by the service provider.

PickSetGo does not separately upload your workout history to provide spoken cues.

6. Children's privacy

PickSetGo is intended primarily for adults and is not specifically designed for children.

The app does not require users to provide their name or date of birth to use its workout features.

If you have concerns about information relating to a child, please contact us.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal information held about you.

You may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

For information stored only on your own device, you can manage your local app data and any backup files you have created.

For personal information received through support or feedback correspondence, you can contact us to exercise your applicable rights.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are concerned about how your personal information has been handled.

The ICO website is https://ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PickSetGo's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be available through the Privacy policy link in the app.
